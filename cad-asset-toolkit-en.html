Pipeline evidence

Each of the three LODs receives rebuilt UVs, normals and PBR textures. The workflow exports PBR + ORM maps and a USD package with relative paths; batch results are 3 SUCCESS, 1 SKIPPED and 0 FAILED.

Geometry inspection

A total of 1,028 bidirectional ray samples were evaluated across four holes and five slots, with a maximum sampled deviation of approximately 0.041 mm. This value represents the defined sample set rather than a global error bound.

VALIDATION SCOPE

This case uses a reproducible synthetic engineering mesh to validate LOD generation, PBR/ORM materials and USD asset packaging. Current validation focuses on geometry, materials and delivery structure; Omniverse Runtime, collision, physics and MDL integration remain future extensions.
