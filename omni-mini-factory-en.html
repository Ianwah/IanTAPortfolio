07 / TECHNICAL DECISIONS

Key technical decisions

Each decision makes the assets more controllable, the scene more maintainable and the resulting data more trustworthy.

01

Scratch safe region

The generation area avoids holes and center slots; evaluated bounds for seeds 0–9 remain within the workpiece.

02

True geometric grooves

Boolean Difference creates real geometry rather than a bump-only illusion, preserving silhouette and occlusion.

03

Reference / Variant

Assets stay independent and are assembled non-destructively in the master stage; the missing-part state changes only one variable.

04

Dedicated capture scene

Capture does not reuse the modeling viewport; a fixed master seed controls EEVEE batch output and reproducibility.
