What the performance figure means

In formal testing, the worst p95 geometry-update time was 2.7974 ms, providing a response-time baseline for parameter changes. Complete scene performance varies with vehicle count, materials and viewport settings.

VALIDATION SCOPE

This case outputs reproducible scene variations with corresponding metadata, focusing on procedural road generation and parameter control. Segmentation, depth, normal and bounding-box annotations remain future data-pipeline extensions.
